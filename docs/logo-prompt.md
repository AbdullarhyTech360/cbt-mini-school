# CBT Mini School — AI Logo Generation Prompt

## Brand Analysis

- **Name**: CBT Mini School (branded as **EA SMS**)
- **Core Identity**: Computer-Based Testing platform for educational institutions
- **Target Audience**: School administrators, teachers/staff, and students
- **Values**: Education, technology, security, reliability, automation, professionalism
- **Functional Purpose**: Online exam creation, delivery, auto-grading, session monitoring, and report generation
- **Tone**: Trustworthy, modern, educational, clean, approachable yet professional
- **Lettermark**: The logo must prominently feature the letter combination **"EA SMS"** as a monogram or integrated lettermark

---

## AI Image Generator Prompt

```
Design a professional, modern logo for "EA SMS" — a Computer-Based Testing platform for educational institutions. The logo is a lettermark that prominently features the letters "EA SMS" as the central design element, integrated with symbolic iconography.

Lettermark design:
- The letters "EA SMS" are arranged in a clean, custom typographic lockup
- "EA" is bold and slightly larger, representing the primary brand identity
- "SMS" is smaller and positioned beneath or beside "EA", acting as a secondary descriptor
- The letters are rendered in a modern geometric sans-serif style with clean, uniform strokes
- The "E" and "A" share a visual connection — their strokes interlock or align to form a unified glyph
- The "S" and "M" in "SMS" are tightly kerned, forming a compact, recognizable block
- Negative space within the letters is used cleverly — the counter of the "A" or the bowl of the "S" subtly contains a small symbol (a checkmark, a shield, or a book icon)

Symbolic elements integrated into or around the lettermark:
- A subtle open book shape forming the background or base of the logo mark
- A thin computer monitor or screen silhouette emerging behind the letters
- A small checkmark or exam completion icon tucked into the letterforms
- A shield or lock silhouette integrated into the negative space of the "E" or "A"

Art style: Modern flat design with geometric precision, minimal strokes, and clean vector aesthetics. No gradients, no photorealism, no 3D effects. The style should feel contemporary like a SaaS product logo — similar to the visual language of Notion, Linear, or modern ed-tech brands.

Color palette:
- Primary: Deep navy blue (#1a2a4a) — conveying trust, professionalism, and academic authority
- Secondary: Bright teal/cyan (#00b4d8) — representing technology, digital innovation, and modernity
- Accent: Warm amber/gold (#f59e0b) — symbolizing achievement, success, and grading excellence
- Background: Transparent or pure white (#ffffff)

Composition:
- The lettermark "EA SMS" is centered and the dominant element
- Symbolic icons (book, monitor, checkmark) are secondary — they enhance but do not overpower the letters
- Balanced negative space around and between the letters
- The design must be legible and recognizable at 32x32 pixels (favicon size)
- Works as a standalone icon and alongside a wordmark

Aesthetic mood:
- Trustworthy and authoritative yet approachable
- Clean, minimalist, and tech-forward
- Conveys reliability and precision (like an exam system should feel)
- Warm enough to feel welcoming to students, professional enough for school administrators
- Modern SaaS product identity — not childish, not corporate-heavy

Additional constraints:
- The letters "EA SMS" must be clearly readable and prominent — not hidden or abstracted beyond recognition
- No drop shadows or excessive effects
- Flat 2D design only
- Vector-style crisp edges
- Works on both light and dark backgrounds
- Suitable for a favicon, app icon, and header branding
```
